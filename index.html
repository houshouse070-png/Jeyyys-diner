Need to know?

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?

Add items to your cart, choose pickup or delivery, complete the demo checkout form, then send the order details to WhatsApp.

Can I pay online?

This demo does not collect payments. Any payment instructions are examples only. A real business would need a verified payment provider integration.

Can I order for pickup?

Yes. Select pickup in checkout. The address and hours currently shown are sample details.

Are the prices real?

No. Both naira and dollar prices are fixed sample portfolio prices and are not exchange-rate conversions.

Can I manage the menu?

The demo admin panel can edit items in this browser. A real shared admin panel requires a secure backend.
